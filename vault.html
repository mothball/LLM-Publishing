The vault expands the monograph archive into its underlying graph: the ten monographs themselves, plus the concepts they develop, the tools they depend on, the architectural decisions they record, the assumptions they rest on, and the unrealized gaps they identify.

Click any node to see its summary and the connections that anchor it. Concepts and tools are reused across monographs; the cross-citations between monographs (already in the prose) show up as edges here.

Tip: the gap nodes (red) are the most useful entry points — they're the open problems still worth working on.
